  <h1>Juhi Singh</h1>

  <!-- I am a DST INSPIRE Faculty in the Department of Bioengineering at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru. My research group focuses on engineering biomaterials for localized disease management, with applications spanning oral infections, cartilage defects, and prosthetic joint infections. We work with a diverse range of materials, from soft hydrogels to mechanically robust bone cements tailored to address specific clinical challenges.
  
Our current investigations emphasize biomaterials for osteomyelitis treatment, cartilage regeneration, and antimicrobial polypeptides. Looking ahead, we aim to expand into tissue regeneration by leveraging 3D/4D bioprinting technologies, integrating AI/ML for biomaterials design, and exploring robot-assisted synthesis of polypeptides to accelerate translational outcomes.

Before joining IISc Bengaluru, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Sydlik Lab, Department of Chemistry at Carnegie Mellon University, USA, where I worked on biomaterials for bone and cartilage regeneration and infection prevention in orthopedic implants. I completed my Ph.D. at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, through the Interdisciplinary Graduate School, co-advised by Prof. Sierin Lim (School of Chemical & Biomedical Engineering) and Prof. Terry Steele (School of Materials Science & Engineering). Prior to that, I earned my Integrated Dual Degree (B.Pharm + M.Pharm) from the Department of Pharmaceutical Engineering & Technology at the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, where I worked with Prof. Brahmeshwar Mishra.

You can learn more about our ongoing projects and research directions on the Research Page.-->
